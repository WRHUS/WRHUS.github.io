ABOUT SARAH VAUGHAN

Sarah Vaughan is a Sunday Times and international best-selling author. Before writing fiction, Sarah spent 15 years as a journalist, including 11 at the Guardian as a political correspondent and news reporter: great preparation for thrillers exploring power, privilege and misogyny, and incorporating police investigations and criminal trials.
